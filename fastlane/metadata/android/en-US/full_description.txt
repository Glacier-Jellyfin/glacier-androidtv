Glacier is a modern Jellyfin client for Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV. It is built for the remote from the ground up, so you find something to watch fast.

Glacier needs your own Jellyfin server (version 12.0 or newer). It does not come with any movies, shows or music.

• Movies, shows and music from your server, with search, sorting, filters, collections and an A to Z rail
• A player made for watching: skip intros, recaps and credits, previews while seeking, chapters and the next episode with a countdown
• The TV switches to the frame rate of the video, so films play without judder
• DTS, TrueHD and more through the built-in FFmpeg audio decoder, and surround sound that stays surround over HDMI ARC
• Several versions of a title, such as 4K and 1080p or a Director's Cut, with the right one picked for your TV
• Live TV with a programme guide, recordings, pause and rewind
• Music that keeps playing while you browse, with a queue, playlists, lyrics and instant mix
• Multiple servers and profiles, Quick Connect and parental controls with age limits and PINs
• Requests through Seerr for titles missing from your library (needs the Jellyfin Enhanced plugin)
• English and German, nine accent colours and a compact layout

Glacier is free and open source under the GPL-3.0. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

Glacier is not affiliated with the Jellyfin project.
