The TV is yours again.

Glacier brings your Jellyfin server into the living room. It was built for the remote from the first day, so the way to your next film stays short.

Glacier needs your own Jellyfin server, version 12.0 or newer. It comes without any films, shows or music.

FOR THE SYSTEM UNDER THE TV
TrueHD and DTS-HD reach your receiver exactly as they are on the disc, even where Android only passes the DTS core or gives up on TrueHD. If nothing gets through, the server converts to E-AC-3 and surround stays.

FOR THE TV ITSELF
Dolby Vision plays even from UHD Blu-rays with profile 7, converted on the device. The TV switches to the frame rate of the film. Before that, Glacier checks with real test files what your TV can play.

FOR EVERYONE ON THE SOFA
Everyone gets a profile with their own picture, colour and progress. The kids profile only shows what suits their age, and the PIN goes in with the D-pad. When a new TV arrives, the settings come along.

WHAT THE REMOTE DOES
• OK skips the intro. Recaps skip themselves.
• Seeking shows preview pictures, so you land on the right scene.
• The next episode is ready before the credits end.
• The A to Z rail jumps straight to the letter, even in big libraries.

THE SHELF NEXT TO IT
• Music with large lyrics in full screen, music videos in the queue and a mini player that stays while you browse
• Photos and home videos by folder, as a gallery or a slideshow
• Live TV with a running preview, a programme guide, recordings, pause and rewind

THE SMALL PRINT
• Search also finds titles that are not on your server yet. Request them through Seerr with the Jellyfin Enhanced plugin.
• Several versions of a title, such as 4K and 1080p or a director's cut, with the right one picked for your TV
• Continue watching and channels on the Android TV home screen (not on Fire TV)
• English, German and Spanish, nine accent colours and a compact layout

Glacier is free and open source under the GPL-3.0. It shows no ads and needs no account.
